03Client material

The clean-up pass

This is the work that used to go to a paralegal. AI catches broken cross-references after heavy negotiation, plus the rest of the tie-out.

Sample output
  • § 9.2 points to § 14.3, which no longer exists
  • "Covered Services" used in § 2 before it's defined in § 4
  • Exhibit C referenced twice, not attached
  • Party name inconsistent: "Provider" vs. "Supplier"

What it takesA check that runs the same way on every draft and is tuned to how your agreements are numbered and defined.

03

The guardrails we build in

The ethics rules didn't change for AI. They now apply to new tools. Every setup we build starts from these three.

Consent before client data goes in

ABA Formal Opinion 512 (2024) calls for informed client consent before representation information goes into an AI tool. It also says boilerplate engagement-letter language isn't enough. Read the opinion.

Every output is a draft

AI does the first pass. You verify it before anything goes out under your name, the same way you'd review a junior associate's work.

Local models for the sensitive work

For privileged material, open models running on your own hardware keep everything on-site. They aren't quite frontier speed, but they're close enough for most drafting and review.
